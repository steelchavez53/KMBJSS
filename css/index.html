Structural Steel Trade for Residential and Commercial Buildings

Structural Steel Contractor Company in California

KMBJ Structural Steel, Inc. is a licensed and certified company with over 20 years of experience. Based in Los Angeles, we serve all of California. Since 2005, we have specialized in structural steel, metal decking, wrought iron, and stainless steel fabrication and installation.

Our skilled team works on new construction, tenant improvements, and renovations. We manage each project from shop drawings and material procurement to on-site erection—always in compliance with ASTM, AISC, and OSHA standards to ensure safety, quality, and full client satisfaction.
